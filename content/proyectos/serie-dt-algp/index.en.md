The DT-ALGP-2026 series brings together working papers on the **governance of fisheries and aquaculture** in Argentina and, in particular, in Tierra del Fuego. Each paper starts from a concrete question (a law, a resolution of the Federal Fisheries Council, an international report or the situation of a species) and answers it with **public sources**, in the open, under a CC BY 4.0 license.

It is published from the UTN Tierra del Fuego Regional School and the Pesqueros en IA community, and shared on ResearchGate and LinkedIn. The papers are written in Spanish.

## Topics

- **Institutions and science:** how the scientific system relates to fisheries management decisions.
- **Regulation:** Tierra del Fuego's aquaculture law and the resolutions of the Federal Fisheries Council.
- **International context:** FAO's SOFIA 2026 report read from the Argentine case.
- **Industry and technology:** naval technology dependence.
- **Species:** southern king crab, from the allowable biological catch to the quota.
